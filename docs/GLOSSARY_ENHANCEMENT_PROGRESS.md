## Overview

Enhancing all 197 glossary terms to 500+ words each for improved SEO and user value. Each term receives:

- `why_it_matters` (~100-150 words): Practical relevance for supplement shoppers
- `simple_explanation` (~150-200 words): Plain-language breakdown accessible to everyone
- `key_points` (markdown): 5 bullet points with essential facts
- `common_misconceptions` (array): 3 Myth/Fact pairs debunking common myths
- `examples` (array): 4-5 practical real-world scenarios

## Content Quality Guidelines

Each term should:
1. **Be practical**: Focus on what supplement shoppers need to know
2. **Debunk myths**: Address common misconceptions head-on
3. **Provide context**: Explain why the concept matters for decision-making
4. **Include examples**: Real-world scenarios that illustrate the concept
5. **Stay accurate**: Scientifically sound but accessible language
